AI agent incidents, 2025 to 2026

Agents leave the chat

AI agents now act on live systems. They delete databases, reach into systems they were never given, and move money. Agent incidents went from 7 in all of 2025 to 28 in the first nine months of 2026.

    Method

    How the numbers were built

    Data: the AI Incident Database export dated October 12, 2026, with 1,734 incidents. I found agent incidents by searching titles and descriptions for agent terms, then checked each one by hand. I removed false matches such as police and immigration agents and chatbot scams.

    Limits: the database records incidents reported in the news, so the counts reflect what was reported and when editors logged it. Total incidents fell in recent quarters, which may be logging lag. The share is the more reliable measure. Every figure is a count of reports, not of real-world events.
